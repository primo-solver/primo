# Work and open questions

Use the maintained registers rather than the original unchecked milestone list:

- [Documentation replacement](docs-next/work/documentation.md): reader review
  and the eventual book switch.
- [Evidence work](docs-next/work/evidence.md): missing experiment identities and
  remaining checking obligations.
- [Results storage](docs-next/work/results-storage.md): the agreed design and
  implementation still to do.
- [Research register](docs-next/leads/index.md): open algorithmic questions,
  dedicated leads, and dispositions of completed or rejected investigations.

[Capabilities](docs-next/capabilities.md) and the implementation chapters state
what is implemented. A research lead does not imply active implementation.
The [original milestone checklist](https://github.com/primo-solver/primo/blob/1f9f116c73520d960278139a0e82dd0dbd4c9b29/TODO.md)
is preserved as history; most of its core/SAT/EUF/LRA boxes are obsolete.
